# Healthy Mezze Social Media Playbook

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This playbook defines how every Healthy Mezze recipe is promoted across social media.

Goals:

- Increase website traffic
- Build brand awareness
- Grow email subscribers
- Encourage recipe saves and shares
- Build a loyal community

Every post should educate, inspire, or help someone cook.

---

# Brand Voice

Always be:

- Friendly
- Helpful
- Encouraging
- Authentic
- Educational

Never:

- Use clickbait
- Exaggerate health claims
- Shame food choices
- Use misleading titles

---

# Platform Goals

## Pinterest

Goal:
Drive long-term website traffic.

Primary KPI:
- Outbound Clicks
- Saves

Content:
- Vertical recipe pins
- Ingredient guides
- Recipe collections
- Seasonal recipes

CTA Examples:

- Get the full recipe
- Save for later
- Try it this week

---

## Instagram

Goal:
Build community and engagement.

Primary KPI:
- Saves
- Shares
- Comments

Content:

- Reels
- Carousels
- Stories
- Behind the scenes

CTA Examples:

- Which version would you try?
- Save this recipe.
- Tag someone who would love this.

---

## Facebook

Goal:
Drive traffic and conversations.

Primary KPI:

- Link Clicks
- Comments

Content:

- Recipe posts
- Cooking tips
- Collections
- Seasonal recipes

---

## YouTube Shorts

Goal:

Introduce the recipe.

Drive viewers to the website.

Keep videos under 30 seconds.

Hook viewers within 3 seconds.

---

## TikTok

Goal:

Entertainment + education.

Quick cuts.

Fast pacing.

Natural voice.

---

# Publish Checklist

Before publishing any recipe:

✓ Recipe tested

✓ Images optimized

✓ Alt text written

✓ SEO title completed

✓ Meta description completed

✓ Internal links added

✓ Structured data verified

✓ Newsletter snippet written

✓ Pinterest pins created

✓ Instagram caption ready

✓ Facebook post ready

✓ Short-form video exported

# Repurposing Strategy

Every recipe should be reused multiple times.

Examples:

New Recipe

Week 3

Healthy Lunch Ideas

Week 6

Top Summer Recipes

Week 10

Mediterranean Meal Prep

Week 16

Editor's Favorites

Never publish once and forget.

# Quarterly Review

Every 3 months review:

Most visited recipes

Highest CTR

Most saved Pinterest Pins

Most viewed Reels

Top keywords

Fastest-growing categories

Then create more content around what performs best.
