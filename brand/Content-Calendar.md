# Quarterly Review Checklist

Every 3 months, review:

- Top-performing recipes
- Lowest-performing recipes
- Most clicked Pinterest Pins
- Most viewed Reels
- Most shared Facebook posts
- Newsletter open rate
- Organic traffic growth

Use this information to plan the next quarter.

---

# Content Planning Rules

✓ Prioritize seasonal recipes.

✓ Publish at least two new recipes every week.

✓ Repurpose every recipe across all platforms.

✓ Update older recipes every 6–12 months.

✓ Create at least one recipe collection each week.

✓ Promote older recipes regularly.

✓ Every recipe should receive multiple rounds of promotion throughout the year.
