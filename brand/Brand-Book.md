# Healthy Mezze Brand Book

Version: 1.0

---

# Brand Overview

Healthy Mezze is a modern food brand dedicated to making authentic Mediterranean cooking simple, healthy, and accessible for everyone.

Our recipes celebrate the rich culinary traditions of Egypt, Lebanon, Greece, Turkey, and neighboring Mediterranean countries while focusing on nutritious ingredients and easy-to-follow instructions.

---

# Mission

To inspire people around the world to cook healthier meals at home by sharing authentic Mediterranean recipes that are delicious, approachable, and backed by wholesome ingredients.

---

# Vision

To become one of the world's most trusted online resources for healthy Mediterranean recipes, cooking inspiration, and nutritional education.

---

# Core Values

## Authenticity

Recipes stay true to Mediterranean traditions while remaining approachable for modern home cooks.

## Health

Recipes emphasize fresh ingredients, balanced nutrition, and sustainable eating habits.

## Simplicity

Cooking should be enjoyable—not complicated.

## Quality

Every recipe is carefully tested, clearly written, and visually appealing.

## Education

Healthy Mezze teaches the story, ingredients, and health benefits behind Mediterranean cuisine.
