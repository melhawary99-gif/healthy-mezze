Phase 5 — Improve Recipe Differentiation

Do NOT rewrite all recipes.

Improve individual recipes where necessary with genuinely recipe-specific information:

cultural context
traditional vs adapted preparation
technique explanations
troubleshooting
substitutions
regional differences
serving traditions
practical cooking knowledge

No generic SEO filler.
